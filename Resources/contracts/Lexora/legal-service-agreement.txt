# LEGAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Between**

**MASON HOTEL**
Hereinafter referred to as **"the Client"**

**and**

**LEXORA**
Hereinafter referred to as **"the Legal Provider"**

---

## 1. PARTIES

This Legal Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **Mason Hotel**, operating as a hotel and hospitality establishment, and **Lexora**, a law firm providing professional legal services.

The Client appoints the Legal Provider to provide comprehensive legal services to Mason Hotel, and the Legal Provider agrees to provide those services in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

---

## 2. PURPOSE OF THE AGREEMENT

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish a comprehensive legal services arrangement under which **Lexora is responsible for all legal matters affecting Mason Hotel**.

Lexora shall provide, manage, coordinate, and oversee all legal services reasonably required to protect the Client's interests, operations, assets, employees, guests, and business affairs.

The Legal Provider's responsibility shall extend beyond general legal advice and shall include the **planning, implementation, supervision, and management of all legal matters affecting the hotel**.

Lexora shall establish and maintain appropriate legal procedures and operational measures designed to prevent, detect, and manage legal risks and incidents affecting Mason Hotel.

The Legal Provider shall perform its responsibilities within the limits of its professional authority, applicable South African law, and the terms of this Agreement.

---

---

## 4. LEGAL FEES AND PAYMENT

### 4.1 Monthly Retainer

The Client shall pay the Legal Provider a monthly retainer fee of **R90,000.00 (Ninety Thousand Rand) per month**, exclusive of applicable VAT.

The retainer covers all legal services provided under this Agreement, including advice, representation, litigation, and all other legal matters.

### 4.2 Invoicing

The Legal Provider shall issue monthly invoices to the Client for the retainer fee.

### 4.3 Payment Terms

The Client shall pay all invoices within **30 calendar days** of receipt.

### 4.4 Annual Review

The retainer fee may be reviewed once every twelve months with **at least 60 days' written notice**.

---

## 5. DUTIES OF THE LEGAL PROVIDER

Lexora shall:

1. Provide professional legal services in accordance with the highest standards of the legal profession.
2. Act in the best interests of the Client at all times.
3. Maintain the highest standards of confidentiality.
4. Avoid conflicts of interest.
5. Communicate promptly and clearly with the Client.
6. Keep accurate records of all legal services provided.
7. Provide advice on legal risk management.
8. Notify the Client of material legal risks or issues.
9. Represent the Client in legal proceedings where required.
10. Comply with all applicable laws and professional standards.

---

## 6. DUTIES OF THE CLIENT

Mason Hotel shall:

1. Provide the Legal Provider with necessary information and documentation.
2. Pay all fees when due.
3. Cooperate with the Legal Provider in the provision of services.
4. Notify the Legal Provider of any material legal issues affecting the hotel.
5. Act on legal advice where appropriate.
6. Designate authorised representatives for legal instructions.
7. Maintain confidentiality of legal advice received.

---

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

Lexora shall keep confidential all information obtained through the performance of this Agreement.

Confidential information includes, but is not limited to:

* Client business operations.
* Financial information.
* Guest information.
* Employee information.
* Legal strategy and advice.
* Commercial information.
* Security information.
* Any other information obtained through the legal relationship.

Such information shall not be disclosed to unauthorised persons except where required by law or professional obligation.

---

## 8. CONFLICT OF INTEREST

Lexora shall avoid conflicts of interest and shall disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest to the Client.

Where a conflict of interest arises, Lexora shall take appropriate steps to manage or resolve the conflict in accordance with professional standards.

---

## 9. TERM AND RENEWAL

### 9.1 Term

This Agreement shall commence on the date signed by both parties and shall remain in force for a period of **twelve (12) months**.

### 9.2 Renewal

The Agreement may be renewed by mutual written agreement of both parties.

---

## 10. TERMINATION

### 10.1 Termination by Either Party

Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing **thirty (30) days' written notice** to the other party.

### 10.2 Immediate Termination

Either party may terminate the Agreement immediately where the other party:

* Commits a material breach of this Agreement and fails to remedy it within a reasonable period.
* Engages in misconduct or fraudulent conduct.
* Becomes unable to perform its contractual obligations.

---

## 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

The parties shall first attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith negotiations between their designated representatives.

If the dispute cannot be resolved through negotiation, the parties may pursue mediation, arbitration, or other lawful dispute-resolution procedures available in South Africa.

---

## 12. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of the **Republic of South Africa**.

---

## 13. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement constitutes the agreement between Mason Hotel and Lexora concerning the provision of legal services.

Any amendment or modification shall be recorded in writing and agreed to by authorised representatives of both parties.
