# SECURITY SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Between**

**MASON HOTEL**
Hereinafter referred to as **"the Client"**

**and**

**IRON WATCH SECURITY SERVICES**
Hereinafter referred to as **"the Security Provider"**

---

## 1. PARTIES

This Security Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **Mason Hotel**, operating as a hotel and hospitality establishment, and **Iron Watch Security Services**, a private security company providing comprehensive security and security-management services.

The Client appoints the Security Provider to provide and manage security services for the Mason Hotel premises, operations, guests, employees, visitors, assets, and other persons or property requiring protection.

The Security Provider accepts such appointment and agrees to assume responsibility for the provision, management, coordination, and oversight of security services at the Client's premises in accordance with this Agreement.

---

## 2. PURPOSE OF THE AGREEMENT

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish a comprehensive security arrangement under which **Iron Watch Security Services is responsible for the overall security of Mason Hotel**.

Iron Watch shall provide, manage, coordinate, and oversee the security measures reasonably required to protect:

1. Mason Hotel and its premises.
2. Hotel guests and visitors.
3. Hotel employees and authorised personnel.
4. Hotel property, equipment, facilities, and other assets.
5. Access points and restricted areas.
6. Hotel operations and areas identified as requiring security protection.
7. Persons, property, and activities reasonably requiring protection while on or associated with the Client's premises.

The Security Provider's responsibility shall extend beyond the deployment of individual security personnel and shall include the **planning, implementation, supervision, monitoring, response, reporting, and management of the hotel's security operations**.

Iron Watch shall establish and maintain appropriate security procedures and operational measures designed to prevent, detect, deter, respond to, and manage security threats and incidents affecting Mason Hotel.

The Security Provider shall perform its responsibilities within the limits of its lawful authority, applicable South African law, and the terms of this Agreement.

---

## 3. SCOPE OF SECURITY SERVICES

Iron Watch shall provide comprehensive security services for Mason Hotel. The services shall include, but shall not be limited to:

### 3.1 Physical Security

1. Protection of hotel premises, guests, employees, visitors, contractors, and property.
2. Continuous security presence at designated areas.
3. Security patrols throughout designated areas of the premises.
4. Monitoring of internal and external areas.
5. Protection of entrances, exits, restricted areas, service areas, parking areas, and other designated locations.
6. Prevention and deterrence of unauthorised activity.

### 3.2 Access Control

1. Control and monitoring of designated entrances and exits.
2. Verification of persons requiring access to restricted areas.
3. Monitoring of visitors, contractors, suppliers, and other non-hotel personnel.
4. Prevention of unauthorised access.
5. Management of visitor and access records where required.
6. Monitoring of access-control procedures.
7. Reporting of attempted or actual unauthorised access.

### 3.3 Surveillance and Security Monitoring

Iron Watch shall assist with the operation and monitoring of security systems designated by Mason Hotel, where such systems form part of the contracted security service.

This may include:

1. CCTV surveillance.
2. Alarm monitoring.
3. Access-control monitoring.
4. Security communications systems.
5. Security-related detection and monitoring systems.
6. Identification and reporting of suspicious activity.
7. Escalation of detected security threats.

Where security systems are owned or supplied by Mason Hotel, Iron Watch shall use such systems in accordance with the Client's procedures and the Security Provider's operational requirements.

### 3.4 Patrol and Inspection Services

Iron Watch shall conduct regular security patrols and inspections of designated areas.

Patrol activities may include:

1. Internal hotel areas.
2. External premises.
3. Parking areas.
4. Entrances and exits.
5. Restricted areas.
6. Service and delivery areas.
7. Perimeter areas.
8. Other areas identified as requiring security attention.

Patrols shall be conducted according to security requirements established by Iron Watch and reasonable operational requirements communicated by Mason Hotel.

### 3.5 Incident Detection and Response

Iron Watch shall detect, assess, respond to, and manage security incidents occurring within the scope of its services.

This includes:

1. Theft and suspected theft.
2. Unauthorised access.
3. Trespassing.
4. Violence or threats of violence.
5. Assaults and disturbances.
6. Vandalism and property damage.
7. Suspicious behaviour.
8. Suspicious objects or activities.
9. Security breaches.
10. Threats to guests, employees, visitors, or property.
11. Other incidents requiring security intervention.

Iron Watch shall take appropriate lawful action within the scope of its authority and shall escalate incidents to Mason Hotel management and relevant authorities where necessary.

### 3.6 Emergency Security Response

Iron Watch shall provide security assistance during emergencies and other situations requiring immediate security intervention.

This may include:

1. Criminal incidents.
2. Fire and evacuation situations.
3. Security threats.
4. Crowd disturbances.
5. Unauthorised entry.
6. Suspicious objects.
7. Threats to life or property.
8. Major security breaches.
9. Emergency access control.
10. Assistance in securing affected areas.

Where appropriate, Iron Watch shall coordinate with the **South African Police Service (SAPS)**, fire services, medical services, emergency services, or other relevant authorities.

### 3.7 Crowd and Event Security

Iron Watch shall provide security support for hotel events, functions, conferences, gatherings, and other activities where security requirements reasonably require additional control or monitoring.

This may include:

1. Crowd management.
2. Access control.
3. Protection of guests and staff.
4. Monitoring of event areas.
5. Prevention of disturbances.
6. Emergency response.
7. Protection of property and restricted areas.

Additional event security beyond the agreed standard service level shall be subject to the commercial provisions of this Agreement.

### 3.8 Asset and Property Protection

Iron Watch shall take reasonable security measures to protect Mason Hotel's:

* Buildings.
* Equipment.
* Furniture.
* Vehicles.
* Facilities.
* Operational assets.
* Restricted property.
* Other assets identified by the Client.

Security personnel shall report suspected theft, damage, loss, or unauthorised use of Client property.

### 3.9 Security Risk Management

Iron Watch shall monitor security conditions at the hotel and identify reasonably foreseeable security risks.

Where appropriate, Iron Watch shall:

1. Identify security vulnerabilities.
2. Recommend reasonable security measures.
3. Report material security risks to Mason Hotel.
4. Recommend changes to security procedures.
5. Adjust security operations in response to identified threats.
6. Assist Mason Hotel in maintaining appropriate security standards.

### 3.10 Security Procedures and Operational Controls

Iron Watch shall develop, implement, and maintain reasonable operational security procedures for the services provided under this Agreement.

Such procedures may include:

1. Access-control procedures.
2. Patrol procedures.
3. Incident-response procedures.
4. Emergency-response procedures.
5. Visitor-management procedures.
6. Key and access-device controls.
7. Security reporting procedures.
8. Communication and escalation procedures.
9. Restricted-area procedures.
10. Other procedures reasonably required for effective hotel security.

### 3.11 Investigations and Incident Follow-Up

Where reasonably required, Iron Watch shall assist with the investigation of security incidents occurring within the scope of its services.

This may include:

1. Reviewing incident records.
2. Gathering available information.
3. Identifying witnesses.
4. Reviewing relevant security records.
5. Preserving available evidence where reasonably possible.
6. Preparing incident reports.
7. Cooperating with law-enforcement investigations.

Iron Watch shall not conduct activities outside its lawful authority.

### 3.12 Security Reporting

Iron Watch shall maintain appropriate records concerning the security services provided to Mason Hotel.

Reports may include:

* Incident reports.
* Patrol reports.
* Access-control records.
* Visitor records.
* Security observations.
* Security risk reports.
* Emergency reports.
* Daily security activity reports.
* Other security records reasonably required by the Client.

---

## 4. SERVICE REQUIREMENTS

Iron Watch shall provide security services at Mason Hotel on a continuous **24-hour basis, 7 days per week, 365 days per year**. The existing contract establishes this continuous coverage requirement.

The Security Provider shall maintain an adequate overall security operation capable of fulfilling the scope of services contained in this Agreement.

Iron Watch shall determine the appropriate deployment of personnel, patrols, monitoring activities, security procedures, and operational controls necessary to provide effective security coverage, subject to Mason Hotel's reasonable operational requirements.

Iron Watch shall ensure that:

1. At least four (4) security officers are on duty at the hotel during any shift.
2. A designated supervisor is on duty or available to oversee security operations.
3. Security personnel are appropriately scheduled to maintain continuity of service.
4. Security coverage is maintained during staff absences, leave, illness, or other personnel shortages.
5. Material changes affecting security coverage are communicated to Mason Hotel.
6. Security operations remain capable of responding to reasonably foreseeable security incidents.

The personnel requirements in this section represent the minimum standard deployment and do not limit Iron Watch's broader responsibility to manage the overall security operation.

---

## 5. SECURITY PERSONNEL

Iron Watch shall recruit, assign, supervise, manage, and discipline all security personnel deployed to Mason Hotel.

All security personnel assigned to the hotel shall remain employees or contractors of Iron Watch and shall **not** be regarded as employees of Mason Hotel.

Iron Watch shall be responsible for:

* Recruitment and vetting.
* Verification of required qualifications and licences.
* Training.
* Supervision.
* Uniforms and identification.
* Shift scheduling.
* Attendance management.
* Personnel discipline.
* Performance management.
* Replacement of unavailable personnel.
* Maintaining appropriate staffing levels.
* Ensuring personnel understand Mason Hotel's applicable security procedures.

Mason Hotel may request the removal or replacement of a security officer where there are reasonable grounds relating to misconduct, incompetence, negligence, unacceptable conduct, or violation of applicable hotel policies.

---

## 6. DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF IRON WATCH

Iron Watch shall be responsible for the overall management and delivery of the contracted security operation.

Iron Watch shall:

1. Provide competent and appropriately trained security personnel.
2. Maintain effective security coverage.
3. Plan and manage security operations.
4. Conduct security patrols and monitoring.
5. Manage access-control operations.
6. Respond to security incidents and emergencies.
7. Maintain adequate supervision.
8. Monitor and assess security risks.
9. Maintain appropriate security procedures.
10. Maintain security records and reports.
11. Report serious incidents promptly.
12. Investigate security incidents where reasonably required and legally permitted.
13. Ensure personnel comply with applicable South African laws and regulations.
14. Ensure personnel comply with reasonable Mason Hotel security procedures.
15. Maintain appropriate communication and escalation procedures.
16. Provide replacement personnel when required.
17. Coordinate with relevant emergency and law-enforcement authorities where necessary.
18. Protect confidential information concerning Mason Hotel.
19. Notify Mason Hotel of material security risks identified during operations.
20. Take reasonable measures to prevent, deter, detect, and respond to security threats.

---

## 7. DUTIES OF MASON HOTEL

Mason Hotel shall:

1. Provide Iron Watch with reasonable access to areas requiring security services.
2. Provide relevant information regarding hotel operations and security procedures.
3. Identify restricted areas and areas requiring additional security attention.
4. Notify Iron Watch of known security risks, threats, or incidents.
5. Provide reasonable facilities necessary for security operations.
6. Designate authorised representatives who may communicate operational instructions to Iron Watch.
7. Cooperate reasonably with Iron Watch in security-related matters.
8. Pay the agreed security service fees in accordance with this Agreement.

---

## 8. ACCESS CONTROL AND PROTECTION OF PREMISES

Iron Watch shall manage and support access control throughout designated areas of Mason Hotel.

Security personnel may:

* Verify authorised access.
* Monitor visitors and contractors.
* Monitor deliveries and service access where required.
* Prevent unauthorised access to restricted areas.
* Maintain visitor or access records.
* Monitor entrances and exits.
* Report suspicious or unauthorised activity.
* Assist with controlling access during emergencies.

Security personnel shall exercise their responsibilities professionally and shall not unnecessarily interfere with legitimate guests, employees, visitors, or authorised personnel.

---

## 9. SURVEILLANCE AND SECURITY SYSTEMS

Where Mason Hotel operates CCTV, alarms, access-control systems, communication systems, or other security technology, Iron Watch shall operate or monitor such systems where those functions form part of the contracted security services.

Iron Watch shall:

1. Monitor designated security systems.
2. Respond to security alerts where applicable.
3. Report detected security incidents.
4. Escalate security alerts where necessary.
5. Maintain appropriate records relating to monitored incidents.
6. Notify Mason Hotel of material security-system failures that affect security operations.

Mason Hotel-owned security equipment shall remain the property of Mason Hotel.

---

## 10. INCIDENT MANAGEMENT AND INVESTIGATIONS

All significant security incidents shall be recorded and reported.

Incident reports should contain, where applicable:

* Date and time.
* Location.
* Persons involved.
* Description of the incident.
* Actions taken.
* Witness information.
* Property affected.
* Police or emergency-service involvement.
* Supporting evidence where available.
* Follow-up actions.

Serious incidents involving threats to life, violence, major property damage, suspected criminal activity, significant security breaches, or other material threats shall be reported to the designated Mason Hotel representative as soon as reasonably possible.

Iron Watch shall preserve relevant security records and information where reasonably possible and legally permitted.

---

## 11. EMERGENCY RESPONSE

Iron Watch shall provide security support during emergencies within the scope of its training, lawful authority, and operational responsibilities.

Emergency security responsibilities may include:

* Security threats.
* Criminal incidents.
* Fire and evacuation situations.
* Unauthorised access.
* Crowd disturbances.
* Suspicious objects or activities.
* Threats to life.
* Threats to property.
* Emergency access control.
* Securing affected areas.

Where necessary, Iron Watch shall coordinate with SAPS, fire services, medical services, emergency services, and other appropriate authorities.

---

## 12. SECURITY RISK MANAGEMENT

Iron Watch shall maintain an active approach to identifying and managing security risks at Mason Hotel.

Iron Watch shall reasonably monitor:

1. Changes in security threats.
2. Vulnerabilities in access points.
3. Suspicious activity.
4. Areas requiring increased patrols.
5. Security incidents and recurring patterns.
6. Risks associated with hotel events and high-occupancy periods.
7. Other circumstances that may materially affect hotel security.

Where a material risk is identified, Iron Watch shall notify Mason Hotel and recommend reasonable corrective measures.

---

## 13. CONFIDENTIALITY AND SECURITY INFORMATION

Iron Watch and its personnel shall keep confidential all information obtained through the performance of this Agreement.

Confidential information includes, but is not limited to:

* Security procedures.
* Security layouts.
* CCTV and surveillance arrangements.
* Access-control information.
* Alarm and security-system information.
* Guest information.
* Employee information.
* Incident reports.
* Security vulnerabilities.
* Emergency procedures.
* Business operations.
* Security assessments and reports.

Such information shall not be disclosed to unauthorised persons except where disclosure is required by law or necessary for the lawful performance of the security services.

---

## 14. DATA AND SECURITY RECORDS

Security records generated in connection with Mason Hotel shall be handled securely.

Where Iron Watch records information concerning guests, employees, visitors, incidents, access activity, or security events, such information shall only be collected, accessed, stored, and disclosed for legitimate security, operational, or legal purposes.

Both parties shall take reasonable measures to protect confidential and personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

---

## 15. SECURITY EQUIPMENT

Iron Watch shall provide the equipment reasonably required for its personnel to perform their security duties unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Such equipment may include:

* Security uniforms.
* Identification.
* Communication equipment.
* Torches.
* Patrol equipment.
* Standard security equipment.
* Other equipment reasonably required for contracted security operations.

Where specific security equipment or technology is supplied by Mason Hotel, such equipment shall remain the property of Mason Hotel unless otherwise agreed.

---

## 16. FEES AND PAYMENT

Mason Hotel shall pay Iron Watch for the security services provided under this Agreement in accordance with the following fee structure.

### 16.1 Service Fee

The parties agree that the monthly security services fee payable by Mason Hotel to Iron Watch shall be **R1,100,000.00 (One Million One Hundred Thousand Rand) per month**, exclusive of applicable VAT.

This fee is based on the following standard deployment:

| Personnel                | Number | Contract Rate | Monthly Total |
| ------------------------ | -----: | ------------: | ------------: |
| Security Officers        |     12 |       R35,000 |      R420,000 |
| Senior Security Officers |      3 |       R45,000 |      R135,000 |
| Security Supervisors     |      3 |       R55,000 |      R165,000 |
| Security Manager         |      1 |       R70,000 |       R70,000 |
| CCTV Operators           |      3 |       R38,000 |      R114,000 |
| Access Control Officers  |      3 |       R38,000 |      R114,000 |
| **Total**                | **25** |               | **R1,018,000** |

**Agreed Monthly Fee: R1,100,000.00**

The parties acknowledge that the agreed monthly fee is **R1,100,000.00**, which includes the personnel deployment above as well as management, supervision, equipment, insurance, and the Security Provider's operational overheads.

The monthly fee is a fixed charge for the agreed security service level and covers ordinary security operations, including personnel deployment, supervision, security management, patrols, monitoring, access control, incident response, reporting, and administrative management.

The fee is not dependent on the identity of specific individuals deployed, provided that Iron Watch continues to satisfy the agreed security requirements.

### 16.2 Additional Services

Mason Hotel may request additional security services where operational requirements increase.

Additional services shall only be provided following written approval by an authorised Mason Hotel representative, except where immediate action is reasonably necessary to respond to an emergency.

Additional services shall be charged at a separately agreed rate.

### 16.3 Overtime and Special Deployments

Services outside the standard service level may be charged separately.

These may include:

* Additional shifts.
* Extended working hours.
* Special events.
* Emergency deployments.
* Temporary increases in security personnel.
* Additional surveillance or monitoring requirements.
* Other security services requested by Mason Hotel.

Applicable charges shall be agreed upon in writing before the additional service is provided, except where immediate action is reasonably necessary during an emergency.

### 16.4 Invoicing

Iron Watch shall issue one invoice to Mason Hotel each month for standard security services and approved additional services.

Each invoice shall identify:

* Invoice number.
* Billing period.
* Monthly service fee.
* Additional approved services.
* Applicable VAT.
* Total amount payable.

### 16.5 Payment Terms

Mason Hotel shall pay all undisputed invoices within **30 calendar days** of receipt.

### 16.6 Invoice Disputes

Mason Hotel shall notify Iron Watch in writing of any disputed invoice amount within **10 business days** of receiving the invoice.

The undisputed portion shall remain payable according to the agreed payment terms.

### 16.7 Annual Price Review

The service fees may be reviewed once every twelve months.

Any increase shall require **at least 60 days' written notice** and the written agreement of both parties.

### 16.8 Taxes

All fees stated in this Agreement are exclusive of VAT.

VAT and any other legally applicable taxes shall be added to invoices where required by South African law.

### 16.9 No Unauthorised Charges

Iron Watch shall not charge Mason Hotel for additional personnel, overtime, equipment, special deployments, or other additional services unless the charge is expressly authorised under this Agreement or approved in writing by an authorised Mason Hotel representative.

---

## 17. SERVICE LEVEL AND PERFORMANCE TERMS

Iron Watch shall maintain sufficient personnel, operational controls, supervision, equipment, and security procedures to fulfil the agreed security requirements.

Iron Watch shall notify Mason Hotel as soon as reasonably possible where a scheduled security position or material security function cannot be maintained.

Iron Watch shall make reasonable efforts to provide a suitably qualified replacement or alternative security arrangement without unnecessary interruption to security coverage.

Repeated failure to maintain the agreed security service level may constitute a service-performance issue and may result in a formal performance review.

---

## 18. PAYMENT DEFAULT

If Mason Hotel fails to pay an undisputed invoice by the applicable due date, Iron Watch shall provide written notice of the outstanding amount.

Mason Hotel shall have **10 business days** from receipt of the notice to remedy the payment default.

Iron Watch shall not immediately suspend essential security services because of a payment dispute.

Where a material payment default remains unresolved after the applicable notice period, Iron Watch may exercise its contractual rights, including suspension or termination of services, subject to applicable law and the termination provisions of this Agreement.

---

## 19. CONTRACT RENEWAL

At least **60 days before the expiry** of the initial contract term, the parties shall review whether they wish to renew the Agreement.

Renewal shall be subject to mutual written agreement.

Any revised:

* Security services.
* Security coverage.
* Personnel deployment.
* Service fees.
* Service hours.
* Responsibilities.
* Security requirements.
* Commercial terms.

shall be recorded in the renewal agreement or an applicable written amendment.

---

## 20. LIABILITY AND INSURANCE

Iron Watch shall maintain adequate insurance coverage for its operations and personnel.

Iron Watch shall maintain public liability insurance with minimum coverage of **R5,000,000.00**.

Mason Hotel shall be entitled to request reasonable proof of insurance coverage.

Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or special damages except where such liability cannot lawfully be excluded.

---

## 21. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform its obligations under this Agreement where such failure is caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

Such events may include:

* Natural disasters.
* Pandemics and epidemics.
* War.
* Terrorism.
* Civil unrest.
* Government action.
* Strikes and labour disruptions.
* Utility failures.
* Other unforeseeable and unavoidable events.

The affected party shall notify the other party as soon as reasonably possible and shall take reasonable steps to resume performance.

Nothing in this section shall prevent either party from taking reasonable measures necessary to protect life, property, or security during a force majeure event.

---

## 22. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing **thirty (30) days' written notice** to the other party.

Either party may terminate the Agreement immediately where the other party:

* Commits a material breach of this Agreement and fails to remedy it within a reasonable period after receiving written notice.
* Engages in serious misconduct.
* Acts unlawfully in connection with the Agreement.
* Becomes unable to perform its contractual obligations.

Upon termination, Iron Watch shall:

1. Return Mason Hotel property in its possession.
2. Provide outstanding security records reasonably required by the Client.
3. Cooperate reasonably with the Client to ensure an orderly transition of security responsibilities.
4. Take reasonable steps to prevent an unnecessary interruption to essential security arrangements during the termination period.

---

## 23. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

The parties shall first attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith negotiations between their designated representatives.

If the dispute cannot be resolved through negotiation, the parties may pursue mediation, arbitration, or other lawful dispute-resolution procedures available in South Africa.

---

## 24. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of the **Republic of South Africa**.

Both parties shall comply with all applicable laws and regulatory requirements relevant to the security services provided under this Agreement.

---

## 25. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement constitutes the complete agreement between Mason Hotel and Iron Watch concerning the provision and management of security services at Mason Hotel.

Any amendment, modification, additional service, or material change to the agreed security arrangements shall be recorded in writing and agreed to by authorised representatives of both parties.
